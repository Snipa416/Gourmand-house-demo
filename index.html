Good to know

Questions, answered

Something else on your mind? Email gourmandhouse@gmail.com.

How do I pay?

By Interac e-Transfer to gourmandhouse@gmail.com. Put your order number in the message so we can match it to your order.

When is my order confirmed?

When we’ve received your e-Transfer and replied by email. If you haven’t heard from us, send us a quick note with your order number.

Where is pickup?

Pickup is in Toronto on Saturdays. We send the address and any instructions with your confirmation email.

What about allergens?

Our cookies contain wheat, milk and eggs. Maple Fudge & Pecan contains pecans, and both flavours are made in the same kitchen, so any of our cookies may contain tree nuts. If you have an allergy, email us before you order.

Can I order a big batch?

Yes. Use the events form or email us with the date and how many cookies you need.
