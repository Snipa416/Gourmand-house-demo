Inside Gourmand House

A good cookie turns any place into a gathering.

Gourmand House began with the kind of cookie people ask about before they've finished the first bite. We bake in small batches, choose ingredients for flavour — not shortcuts — and seal every cookie by hand so it arrives looking like it was meant for you.

Keeping the runs small is the whole point. It means one bake, full attention, and a drop that feels like something arriving rather than something sitting on a shelf.

— Gourmand House, Toronto
