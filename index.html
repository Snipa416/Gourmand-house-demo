    Good to know

    Questions, answered

    Something else on your mind? Email gourmandhouse@gmail.com.

    How do I pay?

    You can pay through our website. Add your cookies to the cart, choose a pickup time and check out. Payment is handled securely by Square.

    When is my order confirmed?

    Your order is confirmed as soon as your payment goes through. Square emails you a receipt right away, and we follow up by email with your pickup details.

    Where is pickup?

    Pickup is Saturday, October 31, 5–9:30 p.m., at It’s OK* Studios, 468 Queen St W, Toronto, ON M5V 2B2. Bring your order confirmation.

    What’s the pickup policy?

    Pickup is only during the pickup window, Saturday, October 31, 5–9:30 p.m. Please come during the pickup hour you chose at checkout, and bring your order confirmation or the name on the order. A friend can pick up for you if they have your confirmation. We can’t hold orders after the window closes, so if you’re running late, email us before it ends.

    What about allergens?

    Our cookies contain wheat, milk and eggs. Maple Fudge & Pecan contains pecans, and all three flavours are made in the same kitchen, so any of our cookies may contain tree nuts. If you have an allergy, email us before you order.

    Is tax included?

    The price you see is the price you pay. We are not charging HST at this time, so a $6 cookie is $6 at checkout.

    When do orders open, and how many are there?

    Orders for this drop open Friday, October 23 at 9:00 a.m. ET and close Thursday, October 29 at 8:00 p.m. ET, or sooner if all 200 cookies sell out. If you miss this one, leave your email in the drop-alert box and you’ll hear about the next drop first.

    Can I order a big batch?

    Yes. Use the events form or email us with the date and how many cookies you need. We need at least 48 hours’ notice.
