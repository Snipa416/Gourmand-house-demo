Our cookies

Handmade, gourmet quality.

Every Gourmand House cookie is made by hand, in small batches, right here in Toronto. We bake with gourmet-quality ingredients — milk and 70% dark chocolate cut into chunks, roasted pecans, maple fudge and sea salt flakes — and we don’t cut corners on any of it.

Each cookie is sealed in its own bag with our logo, so it’s fresh, easy to carry, and ready to share or give as a gift.

    Good to know

    Questions, answered

    Something else on your mind? Email gourmandhouse@gmail.com.

    How do I pay?

    We’re setting up online checkout for our next drop. Orders and payments are not being accepted through this website yet. We’ll show the payment options once checkout is ready.

    When is my order confirmed?

    Ordering is not open yet. When checkout goes live, we’ll make the confirmation steps clear before you place an order.

    Where is pickup?

    Pickup is Saturday, October 31, from 5–9:30 p.m., at Jean Augustine Centre for Young Women’s Empowerment, 101 Portland Street, Etobicoke, ON M8Y 1B1. Bring your order confirmation.

    What’s the pickup policy?

    Pickup is only during the pickup window, Saturday, October 31, 5–9:30 p.m. Bring your order confirmation or the name on the order. A friend can pick up for you if they have your confirmation. We can’t hold orders after the window closes, so if you’re running late, email us before it ends.

    What about allergens?

    Our cookies contain wheat, milk and eggs. Maple Fudge & Pecan contains pecans, and all three flavours are made in the same kitchen, so any of our cookies may contain tree nuts. If you have an allergy, email us before you order.

    When do orders open, and how many are there?

    Each drop is limited. The live count at the top of the menu shows how many cookies are left. When a drop sells out, leave your email in the drop-alert box and you’ll hear about the next one first.

    Can I order a big batch?

    Yes. Use the events form or email us with the date and how many cookies you need.
